Pinterest Editorial Architecture • FAQs

Why 1000 × 1500 resolution?

1000 × 1500 represents the optimal 2:3 vertical aspect ratio mandated by Pinterest's indexing algorithm. Lower resolutions risk compression blurring, while arbitrary heights get truncated in feed feeds.

How does the 100% in-browser generation work?

All typography, tactile Notion cards, shadows, and textures are composited in real-time onto an offscreen HTML5 <canvas> element in your browser memory. Your API keys, concepts, and generated PNG files never touch external storage.

Can I edit headlines before downloading?

Yes. Each card provides an inline quick-editor. Tweak the headline, subtext, or CTA button, and the 1000 × 1500 canvas re-renders immediately.

Pinterest Pin Engine & Frequently Asked Questions

Why test 3 different angles per post?

Pinterest rewards content variety. A listicle pin appeals to organized researchers, a curiosity hook captures casual feed scrollers, and a direct benefit pin converts intent-driven searchers. Testing all three triples your algorithmic reach.

What resolution are these pins?

All pins are rendered at 1000 × 1500 pixels. This matches Pinterest's official 2:3 vertical aspect ratio recommendation and prevents feed compression or pixelation.

How does the BYOK key work?

Your API key is stored only in your private browser localStorage. Direct API calls are made from your client to Google Gemini or OpenAI with zero server tracking or data interception.
